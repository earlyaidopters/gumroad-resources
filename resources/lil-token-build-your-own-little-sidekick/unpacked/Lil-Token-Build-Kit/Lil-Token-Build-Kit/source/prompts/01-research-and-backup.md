# Research and backup prompt

I want to build a personal voice assistant using this device.

Device make and model: [fill in]
Computer and operating system: [fill in]
What I want it to do first: [one small outcome]

Read the official hardware and firmware documentation. Identify the chip, flash capacity, screen, microphones, speaker, camera, motors and sensors. Find the upstream source and its license. Record exact source URLs and revisions.

Inspect the connected device read-only. Do not flash, erase or change settings yet. Explain the current boot and partition layout, what can be changed, and what could prevent recovery.

Propose a backup and restore procedure for this exact device. After I approve writing a backup, save it privately, record its checksum and verify that it can be read. Never add firmware backups, Wi-Fi settings, credentials or raw account data to Git.

Give me a short architecture with the device, local gateway, speech recognition, agent, speech synthesis and first tool. Show where each piece runs and where data goes. Stop and show me the plan before changing firmware.
