## **First Prompt**

```
I'm consulting for a SaaS company that wants to automate their customer onboarding. Their current process: 
1) New signup triggers email sequence
2) Manual sales call scheduling  
3) Demo delivery by human
4) Contract negotiation
5) Implementation handoff

They're losing 40% of prospects between steps 2-3. Budget is $15K. Analyze this workflow, identify the bottleneck causes, and propose 3 specific automation solutions with estimated ROI and implementation complexity.
```

## **Second Prompt**

```
I'm building an AI agent that needs to:
- Process 1000+ daily webhook events from Stripe
- Trigger personalized email sequences via SendGrid
- Update customer data in Supabase
- Generate usage reports

Should I use n8n, Make.com, or Zapier as the backbone? Consider: cost at scale, error handling, maintenance overhead, integration capabilities, and future-proofing. Provide specific reasoning for your recommendation with implementation steps.
```

## **Third Prompt**

```
I want to launch a productized consulting service for small businesses wanting AI automation. Target: companies with 10-50 employees, $1M-10M revenue, struggling with repetitive tasks.

Create a complete go-to-market strategy including: positioning, pricing tiers, service packages, lead generation channels, sales process, and first 90-day launch plan. Make it actionable and specific to my AI consulting background.
```
