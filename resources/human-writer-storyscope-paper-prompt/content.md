## Copy this prompt

Read the attached StoryScope paper and turn its findings about narrative structure into a reusable Human Writer skill. Separate the study’s findings from your own editorial suggestions. When reviewing my drafts, flag places where I overexplain themes, resolve tension too neatly, or spell out a lesson the reader can infer. For each suggestion, quote the passage, explain the issue, and offer the smallest useful edit while preserving my voice, facts, and intended meaning. Cite the relevant paper section for research-based rules. Treat the findings as patterns in this dataset, not universal rules or proof of authorship. Label advice outside fiction as editorial judgment. Ask before rewriting a whole piece. Save the result as a Human Writer skill I can reuse.

## Before you use it

Review the skill Claude creates. The research studies fiction. Applying it to other writing is editorial judgment. This prompt is not an authorship detector and makes no promise of avoiding AI detection.
