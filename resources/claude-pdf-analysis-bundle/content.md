> 
> 1. **Get the Link**:
> 
> - **Direct PDF link**: If you have a direct link to the PDF file, paste it here.
> 
> - **Google Drive link**: If your PDF is on Google Drive, upload it, get the share link, and make sure permissions are set to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.
> 
> 2. **Ask Your Questions**:
> 
> - Type out each question you want answered from the PDF.
> 
> - You can ask multiple questions, and I'll answer each based on the PDF's content.
> 
> Once you’ve pasted the link and listed your questions, I’ll process them using `askClaudeQuestion`.
