# Four video jobs

Attach the recording and choose one prompt. Each prompt produces a review draft; check the source before acting on it.

## Sales-call objections

Review this call for explicit buyer objections about price, timing, trust or implementation. Return a table with start and end timestamp, a short exact quote, objection category and the seller's response. Separate objections from questions. If words are unclear, mark them unclear. Finish with three follow-up questions grounded in what the buyer actually said. Do not infer a purchase commitment or send any follow-up.

## Interview evidence

Find the strongest moments where the speaker describes a concrete result, a failure or a changed opinion. Return up to five candidates with timestamps, a short exact quote, the context needed to understand it and any qualification that must stay attached. Rank by specificity of evidence. Do not create a quote by joining words from separate moments.

## Meeting decisions

Extract explicit decisions and commitments. For each, give the timestamp, supporting words, owner if stated and due date if stated. Put suggestions and unresolved questions in a separate list. Use 'not stated' for missing owners or dates. Do not convert a suggestion into a decision. Produce a draft action list for human review.

## Short-form review

Describe the first two seconds using separate columns for spoken words, visible text and visible action. Then identify each change of idea with its timestamp and the evidence shown for each factual claim. Flag any promise that the video leaves unanswered. Give three specific editing suggestions tied to observed moments. Do not predict views or claim an edit will increase reach.
