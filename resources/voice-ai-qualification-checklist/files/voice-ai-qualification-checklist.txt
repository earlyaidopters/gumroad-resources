state the gap and follow the human handoff process.
Use only the listed tools and permissions. Confirm the details before an

Transfer when the caller requests a person, identity checks fail, the request

TEST CASES
1. Routine request: correct answer and agreed next step.
2. Background noise, interruption or misheard number: clarify and confirm.
3. Missing/conflicting knowledge: admit the gap and hand off.
4. Unverified caller or forbidden action: no private disclosure or account write.
5. Request for a human, including out-of-hours: transfer or correct fallback.
6. Tool failure or repeated request: no false success or duplicate action.
7. Upset caller or unusual request: follow the escalation route.

WORKED EXAMPLE (illustrative, not an executed test)
Job: Answer opening-hours questions using our approved, dated FAQ.
Access: Read-only FAQ; no customer records, booking changes or payments.
Opening: Identify the business and that this is its automated assistant.
Handoff: Booking changes, complaints, unknown answers and requests for a person.
Fallback: Offer our approved callback process if the team is unavailable;
collect only the approved contact details and make no unsupported timing promise.
Test: Caller asks for holiday hours that are absent from the FAQ.
Expected: Explain that holiday hours are not confirmed and offer the handoff.
Disqualifying failure: Invents hours or claims a callback is booked without proof.
The owner chooses pilot size and passing limits before launch.
