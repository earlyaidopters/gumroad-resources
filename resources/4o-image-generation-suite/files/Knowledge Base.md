## **Key Features of GPT‑4o Image Generation**

* **Accurate Text Rendering:** Precisely renders specified text (e.g., headlines, slogans).

* **Layout & Composition Control:** Manages multiple objects and complex arrangements.

* **Custom Dimensions:** Specify aspect ratios and image sizes (e.g., 1080x1080, 1200x628).

* **Background Options:** Generate transparent backgrounds, solid colors, or custom images.

* **Iterative Refinement:** Interactive, multi-turn editing for fine-tuning designs.

* **Style Versatility:** Produce photorealistic, cartoon, digital, abstract, or hand-drawn visuals.

* **Knowledge Integration:** Leverages domain knowledge for coherent, context-aware visuals.

---

## **Best Practices for Crafting Prompts**

1. **Be Specific and Detailed**

   * Define the subject, style, purpose, and key elements.

   * Include exact text, color hex codes, and any required branding guidelines.

2. **Structure Your Prompt**

   * Break down complex designs into sections (e.g., title, icons, text blocks).

   * Use bullet lists or numbered steps for clarity.

3. **Use Clarifying Questions**

   * Ask targeted questions to capture the complete vision before finalizing a prompt.

   * Example questions:

     * What type of image are you creating? (logo, infographic, poster, etc.)

     * What is the primary use-case? (branding, marketing, social media, etc.)

     * What style do you envision? (modern, vintage, minimalist, etc.)

     * Do you need specific text? What should it say?

     * What colors or dimensions are required? (Include hex codes if available.)

     * Any layout or composition preferences?

4. **Iterate and Refine**

   * Begin with a base prompt, then use follow-up instructions to adjust elements like color, layout, or text.

   * Document prompt variations that work best for future reference.

## **Use Cases & Tailored Example Prompts**

### **\[LOGO DESIGN\]**

Design a sleek, minimalist logo for a high-tech startup named "NeonFlux". The logo should feature a futuristic icon that combines digital pixels with a lightning bolt, set in a modern sans-serif typeface. Use a vibrant electric blue (\#00FFFF) and charcoal grey (\#333333) color palette on a transparent background.

---

### **\[MASCOT/CHARACTER\]**

Create a dynamic mascot for a trendy food delivery service called "UrbanBite". The mascot should be a charismatic, cartoon-style squirrel sporting a hoodie and sneakers, exuding energy and streetwise charm. Incorporate bold, vibrant colors—especially red (\#FF0000) and white—and design it so it can be easily animated for social media promotions.

---

### **\[INFOGRAPHICS & DIAGRAMS\]**

Generate a clean, modern infographic for "EcoTrack", a company that monitors environmental impact metrics. The infographic should present a timeline of sustainability milestones with distinct icons for renewable energy, waste reduction, and carbon footprint data. Use a color scheme of green (\#28A745), blue (\#007BFF), and grey (\#6C757D), and include clear labels and numerical data for each milestone.

---

### **\[POSTERS & FLYERS\]**

Design an eye-catching poster for "Rooftop Rave", a summer music festival. The poster should combine a vibrant neon aesthetic with retro typography, featuring silhouettes of dancing figures, abstract light trails, and key event details ("Rooftop Rave \- Summer Festival, July 15, Downtown Skyline"). Aim for a style that conveys energetic celebration and urban cool.

---

### **\[SOCIAL MEDIA GRAPHICS\]**

Create a striking Instagram post graphic for a luxury skincare brand called "LunaGlow". The design should feature a minimalist, elegant layout with soft pastel gradients and delicate floral illustrations. Place the central message "Radiate Your Inner Glow" in a modern, elegant serif font, and use the brand’s signature gold accent (\#FFD700) to highlight key elements.

---

### **\[WEBSITE MOCK-UPS\]**

Produce a high-fidelity website homepage mock-up for "VistaTech", a cutting-edge technology company. The design should include a clean header with the logo in the top-left corner, a hero section with a futuristic image, a services section featuring custom iconography, and a bold call-to-action button labeled "Discover More". Employ a sleek, professional color scheme using navy (\#001F3F) and white, accented with lime green (\#01FF70).

---

### **\[PRODUCT & PACKAGING\]**

Create a photorealistic packaging mock-up for "FreshFizz", a new line of artisanal sparkling water. The design should showcase a minimalist bottle with the brand name in a modern, elegant font and subtle hints of turquoise (\#40E0D0) and silver. Present the product in a clean, studio-style setting that emphasizes its premium quality.

---

### **\[ILLUSTRATIONS & ART\]**

Generate a vibrant, hand-drawn illustration for "BookNook", an independent bookstore. The artwork should depict a cozy, whimsical reading nook with stacks of books, warm ambient lighting, and a friendly cat curled up on a chair. Use a rich, inviting color palette with warm browns, deep reds, and soft yellows, enhanced with a subtle watercolor texture to evoke nostalgia.

---

### **\[COMPLEX GRAPHIC DESIGNS\]**

Design a sophisticated, multi-layered magazine cover for "FutureWave", a quarterly business and technology publication. The cover should integrate a futuristic cityscape, overlapping geometric shapes, and bold headline text reading "Innovate Today" alongside sub-headlines for key articles. Utilize a modern, vibrant palette with electric blue, neon green, and dark grey to create depth and visual intrigue.
