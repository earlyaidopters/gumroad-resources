**Prompt 1**

```
Create a comprehensive disruption analysis of how humanoid robots will completely transform home services industries between 2025-2030. This analysis should include:

1. **Industry Death Clock**: Interactive timeline showing when specific service industries will face 50%, 75%, and 90% automation (cleaning services, lawn care, home maintenance, elderly care, pet services, food delivery)

2. **The $38 Billion Robot Economy Breakdown**:
- Detailed market analysis of the humanoid robot industry growing from $1.55B to $38B by 2035
- Cost analysis showing robot pricing dropping from $150K to under $30K
- ROI calculations for robot ownership vs human labor costs

3. **Business Model Revolution Scenarios**:
- "Robot-as-a-Service" subscription models for home cleaning
- Franchise opportunities in robot fleet management
- Insurance and maintenance service ecosystems
- Human-robot hybrid service companies

4. **Interactive Impact Calculator** that estimates:
- Job displacement numbers by zip code and service type
- New job creation in robot maintenance, programming, and oversight
- Economic impact on local service economies

5. **Startup Opportunity Map**: Identify 15 specific business opportunities in the humanoid robot home services ecosystem, with investment requirements and projected returns

6. **The Human Resistance**: Analysis of which home services will remain human-dominated and why

Include real case studies from companies like 1X (Neo robot), Tesla (Optimus), and current home service market leaders. Create interactive visualizations showing before/after scenarios for typical neighborhoods and economic projections for early adopters vs. late adopters
```
