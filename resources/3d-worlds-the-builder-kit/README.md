## About

### What You Get

- 
Play the example: Open Inside Codex in your browser and explore the learning world from the video.

- 
The original plan: The retained 15-section production plan, with a link to the annotated walkthrough and clear provenance notes.

- 
The build requests: 20 retained development requests showing how the idea, quality expectations, tour and refinements evolved. Login and administrative material is excluded.

- 
Eight reusable prompts: Scope your idea, write the plan, finish one lesson, expand the world, test it, host it, add a tour and maintain it.

- 
The full guide: A 10-page PDF plus matching Markdown you can feed to your AI to prime it for the work.

- 
Editable templates: A world brief, mission worksheet, asset register and release record you can adapt to your own subject.

- 
Testing tools: A release checklist and reproducible defect report template for checking function, teaching and presentation.

- 
Start here: Try the live game, fill in the world brief, then attach the guide and original plan to your coding agent. Use the prompt pack one stage at a time.

- 
Free access: Enter $0 to download the complete kit. An optional contribution is welcome. No community membership is required.

- 
Scope: This kit provides live game access and the planning method. It does not include the private full game source repository or third-party subscriptions. Your own tools, generation and hosting may have costs.
