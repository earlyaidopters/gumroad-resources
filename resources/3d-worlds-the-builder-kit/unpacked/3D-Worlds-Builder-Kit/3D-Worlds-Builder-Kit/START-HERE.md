## Build your own version
1. Read `05-guide/BUILD-A-LEARNING-WORLD.md` or the matching PDF.
2. Fill in `03-templates/WORLD-BRIEF.md` with one audience, one topic and one useful learning outcome.
3. Attach the guide, your brief and `01-original-material/GAME-PRODUCTION-PLAN.md` to your coding agent. Paste `02-prompts/01-prime-and-scope.md`.
4. Use the numbered prompts one stage at a time. Review the plan, then finish one playable lesson before expanding the world.
5. Test locally, publish to a host you control, and test the actual public URL.
