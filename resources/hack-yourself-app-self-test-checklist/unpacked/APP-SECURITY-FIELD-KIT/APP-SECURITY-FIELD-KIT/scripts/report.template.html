How ratings work

Ease runs from 1 (unusual access or difficult conditions) to 5 (public, repeatable action with few barriers). Consequence runs from 1 (limited nuisance or information) to 5 (broad compromise or severe impact supported by evidence). Explain both in the rating reason.

Grid color uses ease × consequence: 1–4 lower, 5–9 moderate, 10–15 elevated, 16–25 highest. The urgent list uses its own rule shown above. These are simple triage choices, not CVSS or an industry severity standard. Unknowns stay off the grid. Lists provide the same findings without requiring use of the plot.
