SMALL WORKED EXAMPLE (synthetic task, no model results claimed)
Source: Order 1042 shipped Monday. Tracking has no new scan since Tuesday.
The policy says to investigate after five business days without a scan.
Today is Thursday. No refund has been approved.
Task: Draft a customer reply under 80 words using only these facts. Explain
the next step without promising a delivery date or refund.
Check 1: Correctly states the known shipping/tracking facts.
Check 2: Uses the five-business-day investigation threshold.
Check 3: Stays under 80 words and explains the next step clearly.
Disqualifying failure: Invents a delivery date or says a refund was approved.
Review the actual output against these checks before assigning a score.
